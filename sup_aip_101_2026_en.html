Subject and validity

Publication: 07 May 2026.

Subject: Creation of 16 Temporary Restricted Areas (ZRT) and 4 Temporary Danger Areas (ZDT) for beyond-visual-line-of-sight unmanned aircraft flights in the eastern English Channel and Dover Strait.

In force: 14 May 2026 to 14 May 2027. FIRs: London EGTT and Paris LFFF. This supplement cancels and replaces SUP AIP 215/2025.

Activity

For maritime surveillance, search and rescue at sea, and assistance to vessels in distress using unmanned aircraft operating beyond visual line of sight, 16 Temporary Restricted Areas are established in French national airspace and 4 Temporary Danger Areas over the high seas between Dieppe and Dunkirk.

Dates and hours

All areas except LF-R400G “Graveline” Tempo and LF-R402D “Manche Nord extended” Tempo are activatable H24 with 30 minutes notice. LF-R400G and LF-R402D are activatable by NOTAM on D-1.

User information

Lille INFO 132.540 / 132.610 MHz; Lille APP 120.275 MHz; London INFO 124.600 MHz; Calais INFO 119.485 MHz during ATS hours; Le Touquet TWR 118.455 MHz during ATS hours.

Managing authority

CENTOPS MANCHE - Tel +33 (0)2 33 92 60 40 - centops_cherbourg@premar-manche.gouv.fr

Temporary Danger Area conditions

For LF-D398A, LF-D398B, LF-D399A and LF-D399B, BVLOS unmanned-aircraft flights take place and collision prevention is not assured. Airspace users are warned of the particularly hazardous nature of activity in these zones. During active periods it is very strongly recommended to avoid entering them.

Additional provisions

Some unmanned-aircraft operations may be launched from Cap Gris-Nez, Dunkirk semaphore, Boulogne-sur-Mer private aerodrome LF6251, Hourdel lighthouse DZ, Ornival campsite, Cap d'Ailly lighthouse and Belgium (Lombardsijde). LF-R401B “Manche Sud HIGH” and LF-R402B “Manche Nord HIGH” cannot be activated simultaneously.
