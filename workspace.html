#LATLONPLOT

Multi-point NOTAMs auto-fill from the pasted or selected NOTAM. The points are plotted as one area (polygon) with a single numbered pin at its centre; untick PLOT to leave a point out.

HAZARD TRIGGER: Multi-point hazards generate a HAZPLOT alert only when an aircraft is within 1 NM of the polygon boundary and within the configured vertical limits.

Click anywhere on the map to set the coordinates above.

Start and Finish are optional. Leave both blank to keep the hazard active until it is edited or deleted. When used, NOTAM times are UTC/Zulu.

With times entered, the pin appears 30 minutes before Start, flashes until Start, remains steady until Finish, then hides. Without times it remains active until edited or deleted.
